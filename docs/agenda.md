# Agenda: what Novalie 1.0 can do, and how it was measured

*Abdallah Alramahi (Abdallah1999alramahi@gmail.com)*

Every point below is built and measured. A world marked *sealed* was written after the code was frozen by a hash
(the world refuses to run on other code, and its seeds come from the hash), and its first result is the one reported.

## A. Five abilities

| | Ability | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Concepts from experience (what "heavy" means, from what lifting does) | classes of symbols by the shortest description of what acts do to them: light, medium and heavy bodies exactly; charges and coins exactly |
| A2 | Causal laws, used on things never seen | the cause comes first (the act, or what came before), not what comes with it; a new body takes the laws of its class |
| A3 | Foresight before acting | every sense foreseen before each act: bodies 3/3, charges 3/3 |
| A4 | Transfer to a new domain, with a gain | a machine it invented with three cups is a part of its alphabet with three dancers (other names, buttons reversed): found after 25 presses in 3 of 3 seeds, against 40, 40 and 50 for a new model |
| A5 | Continual learning | Page's test on every law; when a law changes it recounts from the act it began to fail: 81% after 100 acts (a new model 71%), then 96-99% |

## C. Deeper properties

| | Point | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Choosing what to learn | the act whose outcomes differ most between the classes a new thing may be (Jensen-Shannon): a new metal classed in one act, none in eight without |
| C2 | Knowing when it does not know | calibration over 12,600 forecasts: error 0.026 |
| C3 | New concepts nobody programmed | quantities of its own (least, most, sum of other senses): the narrowest passage of a flow, the outlet from 83.4% to 100% |
| C4 | Learning to learn | what it invents becomes a part of its alphabet: the next world of the kind is learned from fewer presses (A4) |
| C5 | Knowing the limits of its model | it says when its laws broke and from which act; no false alarm in stable or control worlds |
| C6 | Representing a problem many ways | for each kind of outcome its own precision (the whole situation, its own value, with another sense, how long since a change); the shortest wins |
| C7 | An experience of information | it senses its own body and mind (its thread's work, its memory, its inventor, its workshop, what it learned, what its reading brought) as senses with laws of their own, foresees them and acts on them. In its real life: laws of what its acts do to itself (reading, not waiting or thinking, brings its reading: odds 0.46 against none), every forecast of itself that fell due came true, and its drive came and broke as what its acts brought changed (H1) |

## G. Many brains, full control of itself

| | Point | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Brains Nova(1), Nova(2), ... that think together | each site belongs to one brain (rendezvous hashing); meanings proved from all their counts together; a brain is born when the last one proved something and the computer has room, let go when it does not read or memory is short. ## H. Four stages toward intelligence

| | Stage | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Responding electronically to its surroundings | its surroundings are its computer and its network; its acts are to wait, to think and to read (a page asked at once and waited for a moment). In its real life its law of effect found that reading brings it pages and waiting or thinking never does, and it read by that law (306 of 306 acts after a restart, its clock within 3 seconds of the real one; 2,924 reads in an hour-long run); its network turned off, the law broke within 80 seconds (the fewer pages its reading was bringing, the longer the run of nothing its rule needs) and its acts took turns; the network back, the law returned within 80 seconds |
| H2 | A nervous system: parts woken by evidence | its judge of laws wakes the act a near candidate reaches its rule, not at the next tick of a clock: a weak law found at act 10,694, 9,007, 9,172 against 11,536, 9,535, 9,535, with 6 to 10 wakes; concepts by an anytime-valid test (Ville): metals 3/3, mixtures 3/3, no false concept in 30 misleading worlds |
| H3 | Integration: one currency, a drive of its own | one state, one loop, one rule, and one currency, digits of evidence, for laws, episodes, twins and its drive; laws of two things together (on the graphics card): 89.4% where any one condition gives 56%. Its drive is its own: in its real life it came from what its acts brought (two rates told apart by the one rule), and broke and came back with its network (H1) |
| H4 | Self-awareness, memory, other minds | a journal of every belief (when, by which tool, from whom, and its fate); forecasts of itself checked when they fall due; what changed since you last saw it; what each of its brains told it and what held. In its real life, over ten starts: sure of the second again 5 seconds into every restart (49 at its first start), of the minute in 170; every forecast of itself that fell due came true; no belief about its control in any start |

## T. New methods

| | Point | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| T2 | Its heavy work on the graphics card | the inventor's search on the card (CUDA C compiled by NVRTC, the same search as the processor): 8.03 billion mechanisms in 33.8 s of card time; two-condition laws, laws of counts and concepts on the card; every decision taken on the processor, exactly |
| T3 | New methods for what was missing | episode cells, clocks, nerves, twins, the law of effect (an act's outcome counted while the act lasts; two rates told apart by universal inference; a run-length refutation), curiosity judged by what it tells, new parts of its alphabet, a library of parts it invented: each measured above |

| | Point | Measured |
|---|---|---|
